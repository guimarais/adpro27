## Why

Students need a simple mental model of frontend/backend/API roles and a reliable way to consume JSON. The approved class-04 interview narrowed the required scope to one small Requests client, a supplied FastAPI service, explicit failures, and short OpenAI/Ollama HTTP previews. Pagination and retry implementation move to optional work.

## What Changes

- Add five ordered, independently executable notebooks and six explained exercise references under classes/04-consuming-apis.
- Supply the earlier Observation/Report package, a tiny local FastAPI route, a small Requests boundary, and a two-country/one-year World Bank adapter.
- Supply saved success/failure inputs, a real public-data snapshot, illustrative AI envelopes, and optional synthetic pagination pages.
- Keep notebook/reference execution offline and credential-free. Real local/public/model calls require explicit terminal commands or live switches.
- Preserve 90 classroom and 210 independent minutes, with at most 180 optional minutes.
- Update the blueprint to the accepted simplified scope and record automated, live, static, and desktop evidence separately.
